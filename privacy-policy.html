Privacy Policy

Sales Guru Marketing Agency

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Sales Guru Marketing Agency ("we", "our", or "us") built the Sales Guru app (package com.salesguru.app). This page explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

The Sales Guru app has no user accounts and no backend database. We do not collect or store any personal data on our servers. The only information the app handles is what you choose to type into its forms:

  • Booking form — your name, the service you are interested in, your budget and any project details
  • Technical support form — your name, phone number (optional) and a description of the issue

When you tap send, the app prepares a WhatsApp message containing this information. Nothing is sent until you choose to send that message in WhatsApp. The app does not keep a copy of it.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files — the app requests no device permissions
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no advertising ID
  • No payment information

How We Use Your Information

Messages you send us are used only to reply to your enquiry or support request. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because the app stores nothing, uninstalling it removes everything on your device. Messages you send us remain in our WhatsApp chat history. To have them deleted, email info@salesguru.co.zw or message us on WhatsApp and we will delete them within 30 days.

Third-Party Services

The forms and chat button open WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.), which handles your message under its own privacy policy. The app also contains links that open our Facebook and Instagram pages, your phone dialler and your email app. Those services have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Security

All links the app opens use secure HTTPS connections. Since we do not store your data, there is no app database that could be exposed.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
