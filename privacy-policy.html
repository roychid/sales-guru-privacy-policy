Privacy Policy

Sales Guru Marketing Agency

Last updated: March 2026

Sales Guru Marketing Agency ("we", "our", or "us") built the Sales Guru app. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal data on our servers. The Sales Guru app operates without a backend database. The only information processed is:

  • Your name and phone number — entered voluntarily in the booking form and sent directly to our WhatsApp via a secure link
  • Budget and service preference — included in the WhatsApp message you send

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted via the booking form is used solely to respond to your service inquiry. It is sent directly to our WhatsApp and is not stored, shared, or sold to any third party.

Third-Party Services

The app uses WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) to facilitate communication. By using the booking feature, you are redirected to WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review WhatsApp's privacy policy at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Links to Other Websites

The app may contain links to external websites. We have no control over the content or privacy practices of those sites and assume no responsibility for them.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
